Our story

Rooted in care since 1990.

Established in 1990, Muskan Nursery is a well-recognized and trusted manufacturer and supplier of a diverse range of high-quality plants and lawn grass solutions. Operating as a proprietorship firm, we specialize in indoor plants, decorative plants, flowering plants, lawn grass and fruit plants.

Based in Kolkata, West Bengal, we have built a reputation for healthy, well-maintained and premium-quality plants. Under the leadership of Mr. Mijanur Molla, our dedicated team ensures proper plant care, packaging, handling, timely delivery and customer satisfaction — with a loyal customer base across Pan India.
